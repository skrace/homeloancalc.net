Privacy Policy

Last Updated: July 2026

This privacy policy outlines how we collect, store, use and protect visitor data when you access our mortgage calculator website. This policy complies with the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) and the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

1. Data We Do NOT Collect

Our core mortgage calculator runs fully client-side inside your browser. We never collect, store or upload any personal financial inputs you type into the calculator (home price, income, loan amounts, credit details). No loan data is sent to our servers at any time.

We do not require account registration, email sign-ups, or phone number submission to use all website tools.

2. Automatically Collected Anonymous Data

When you visit our site, our CDN, analytics and advertising partners may automatically collect anonymous non-personal data including:

  • Visitor country, region and device type
  • Browser version, screen resolution
  • Page visit time, session length, clicked links
  • Anonymous IP address (partially masked for privacy)

3. Cookie Usage

This website uses first-party and third-party cookies for two core purposes:

  1. Functional Cookies: Temporary browser storage to remember minor calculator interface preferences.
  2. Advertising/Analytics Cookies: Google AdSense, Ezoic and Google Analytics deploy cookies to serve relevant financial advertisements and track anonymous site performance statistics.

You may disable all cookies via your browser settings at any time, though this may impact ad display and calculator minor interface features.

4. Third-Party Service Providers

We share anonymous visitor metrics with trusted third-party platforms: Google (Analytics & AdSense), Cloudflare CDN, Chart.js and Tailwind CSS CDNs. All third parties are bound by their own privacy policies and cannot extract your personal identifying information.

5. User Data Rights (CCPA / GDPR)

  • The right to request deletion of all stored anonymous visitor records
  • The right to opt out of targeted interest-based advertising
  • The right to view all non-personal data linked to your past site visits

Submit all data requests via our Contact page.

6. Data Security

Our entire website runs over encrypted HTTPS connections via Cloudflare global CDN. All visitor traffic is encrypted to block unauthorized third-party interception of browsing activity.

7. Policy Updates

We reserve the right to revise this privacy policy at any time. Major changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date.
