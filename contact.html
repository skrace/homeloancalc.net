Contact Us

Get In Touch

We welcome all visitor inquiries, including calculation bug reports, CCPA/GDPR privacy data requests, website feedback, and general mortgage tool support messages.

Email Support

All legal, technical and feedback requests can be sent to our official contact email: contact@homeloancalc.net

Please replace this email with your custom domain email if you own one.

Message Guidelines For Faster Replies

  • Math calculation errors: Attach your input values and expected monthly payment result.
  • Privacy data deletion requests: Clearly state your rights under CCPA or GDPR.
  • Advertising feedback: Mention the ad position on the mortgage calculator page.
  • Legal policy questions: Specify the page (Disclaimer / Privacy Policy) your query refers to.

Response Time

Standard inquiries will be reviewed within 2–4 business days. Complex legal or technical requests may take up to 7 business days for full replies.
